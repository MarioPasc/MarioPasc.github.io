I am an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, and I work on Bayesian deep learning for deep generative models. My Master's thesis, supervised by Daniel Hernández-Lobato, is on scalable linearised Laplace approximations to quantify the uncertainty of pretrained flow-matching models.

Since 2023 I have been a research trainee in the Computational Intelligence and Image Analysis Group (ICAI) at the University of Málaga, where I work with Ezequiel López-Rubio on deep learning for brain MRI and invasive coronary angiography, and on representations of graphs as sequences of instructions for symbolic regression. I received a BSc in Biomedical Engineering (bioinformatics track) from the University of Málaga in 2026. I intend to pursue a PhD on Bayesian methods for generative models, in particular diffusion and flow matching.
